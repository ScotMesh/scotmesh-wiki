## You found a Mesh repeater

You've found a [LoRA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LoRa) mesh repeater. Probably setup by one of the ScotMesh volunteers. 

MeshCore / Meshtastic are offgrid wireless communication protocols using the 868MHz frequency band. They are useful for communicating where mobile phone signals can't reach or during times when the mobile phone system fails.

The repeater is a self contained device that needs minimal maintainace, and we do ask that people who install them get permission before they place them on someone elses land.

Please contact us either via [Discord](https://discord.gg/ytxfyuDmSt) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/26406719398968062) if you would like to know more about the device you found.
ScotMesh has no control over where a MeshCore user installes their repeater, but we may be able to track down who has installed it.
